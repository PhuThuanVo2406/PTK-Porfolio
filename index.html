What we do for you

Recognized. Prepared. Ready to fly.

Think of us as your launch crew. Whatever you're aiming for, whether that's a transfer, med school, a first internship, or just finding your people, here's how we help you get there.

01

We recognize your talent.

Your grades earned you an invitation, and we make sure it counts: a medallion, an induction ceremony, and an honor that four-year schools recognize on your applications.

02

We help you pay for what's next.

Phi Theta Kappa members can apply for transfer scholarships at more than 800 partner colleges and universities. Our VP of Scholarship walks you through them, one application at a time.

03

We grow you as a leader.

Run a meeting, lead a project, speak up at a conference. Here, leadership is something you practise with people cheering you on, not just something you read about.

04

We take you places.

Honors Institutes and leadership conferences from Denver to College Station, where you'll meet students and mentors from across the state and the country.

05

We give back together.

Service and Honors in Action projects let you turn what you're learning into real help for HCC and for Houston, and they make great stories for your interviews.

06

We've got your back.

Study buddies, food-truck nights, and officers who actually answer your messages. College is a lot. You don't have to do it alone.

About

Fifty years of students raising the bar at HCC.

Omega Sigma was chartered in 1973 at what is now Houston City College – Northwest. More than 12,600 students have joined since, each invited for their academic achievement and asked to do something with it.

Recent chapter projects include research on how access to green space in Houston affects mental health and well-being, filming a virtual tour of campus, and a community project with Houston Community College.

The chapter is guided by advisors Dr. Nichole Boutte-Heiniluoma, Dr. Gisela Ables, and Dr. Nicolas Rangel, whose work has earned national advisor awards of its own.

Your year with us

From your first meeting to the conference floor.

  1. 01

    Get inducted

    Accept your invitation, get your medallion, and meet the officers and advisors who will be in your corner all year.

  2. 02

    Show up

    Drop into general meetings for workshops, guest speakers, scholarship tips, and food. Come as often as your schedule allows.

  3. 03

    Go further

    Join us at Honors Institutes and leadership conferences in places like Denver and College Station. You'll come back with new skills and new friends.

  4. 04

    Make your mark

    Lead a service or research project, step into an officer role, and leave HCC with a story worth telling on every application.
